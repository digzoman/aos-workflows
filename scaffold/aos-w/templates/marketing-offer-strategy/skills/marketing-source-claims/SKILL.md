# Source truth skill (just in time)

Read at the referencing task only. Record precise source, access date, reliable observed claim, and limitations. Label competitor offer `OBSERVED_COMPETITOR`; our own unverified concept `HYPOTHESIS`; only independently owner-confirmed offer/terms `VERIFIED_DELIVERABLE`. Never infer our product capability or authorized publish status from a competitor advertisement, ad performance, a proposed price, or a generated image. Do not reproduce customer private data, access keys, actual ad account identifiers or internal messages in public records. Unknown access means UNKNOWN, not a verified empty result. Skill instructions provide no extra permissions.
