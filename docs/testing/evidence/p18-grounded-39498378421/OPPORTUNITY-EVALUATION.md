# Bucket 9 competitor evaluation, public websites as of 2026-10-20

Coaches and consultants. Tadka advertises a self-serve creative system, Oak Marketing an AI assisted ads framework and The Ads Girl consulting and managed ads. This is the most direct pilot buyer for a beginner toolkit, but actual demand is unmeasured.

Agency owners. Madgicx and AdCreative.ai sell established tools. Likely higher operator literacy and repeat volume, but the toolkit needs clear differentiation and workflow integration.

Remodelers. V Easy, Syntex and NorthVale primarily offer done-for-you lead generation. High-value inquiries and available imagery are attractive, but a self-managed 47 dollar toolkit has unproven fit.

Ranking is a test hypothesis, not statistical scoring. Evidence confidence is low to moderate. Eight first party supplier webpages were observed, zero active Meta ad cards were verified. Business results and independent customer demand are not proven.
